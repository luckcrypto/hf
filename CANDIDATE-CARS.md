# Candidate cars — shortlist, not yet confirmed

Cars that probably belong on hypercars.fyi but are **not on the site yet**, because
their figures could not be verified to the standard everything else is held to.

Nothing here is live. A car moves out of this file and into
`build/data/hypercars.json` only once its power, and ideally its weight, speed and
0–60, can be traced to a manufacturer figure. Where a car has a genuinely
unpublished number (the RB17 has no quoted 0–60, the Brouillard no published top
speed), that is fine — it ships with a `—`. What is not fine is inventing one.

## Where the gaps keep coming from

Two patterns, both worth knowing:

1. **Track-only variants.** Manufacturers spin these off constantly and they rarely
   get the coverage the road car does — the Valkyrie AMR Pro sat missing for months.
2. **2025–26 launches.** Anything announced after early 2026 will not be in my
   training data, so it only surfaces if we go looking. The RB17 ran publicly for
   the first time weeks ago; the Denza Z was revealed at Goodwood this year.

Worth re-running a gap check every month or so rather than waiting to notice.

## Deliberately excluded

- **Devel Sixteen** — claimed 5,000 hp, no credible evidence of a functioning car.
- Anything that exists only as a render or a press release with no running prototype.
